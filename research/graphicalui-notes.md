# graphicalui.com: visual notes (studied 2026-11-12, 1440px and 390px)

- Page: pure white main column (#ffffff) inside a #f7f7f7 body; no texture, no gradients, almost no color outside product shots.
- Ink is near-black #171717, never pure black. Secondary text is a warm grey (#7e7e7a, #65615e); nav links sit at 30% ink.
- Type: one licensed neo-grotesk (GT Standard) at weight 500 for headlines, 400 for body. Headlines 48/64 desktop, 32/40 mobile, slight negative tracking (-0.5px at 20px).
- Body copy is large (20/32) and grey; each item pairs an ink title line with a grey description directly under it, no gap.
- Mono (Geist Mono, ui-monospace) only for commands, code and data readouts.
- Radii: 12px for controls and cards, 999px for pills and segmented controls, 16-24px for big framing panels; inner radius = outer minus padding.
- Surfaces: light grey fill #eeeeee / #f0f0ee for inputs, chips and inactive tiles; hairline borders around grouped content.
- Grouping: one rounded container split into cells by 1px dividers, instead of separate floating cards.
- Inverted panels: dark #171717 / #242424 panels (theme inspector, terminal, pricing) sit next to white content for contrast; text on them is #eeeeee.
- Elevation: none on most things; a single very large, very soft shadow under the main showcase frames.
- Layout rhythm: centered narrow text column (~640px), wide showcase (~960-1300px), about 160-200px of air between sections.
- Section headings are centered, two short lines, sentence case, ending with a period.
- Feature lists: 2-column grid, line icon, ink title, grey text; generous row gaps (~100px).
- FAQ and lists use hairline rules between rows, chevrons at the right, no boxes.
- Nav: two quiet text links top right, no bar, no border.
- Mobile keeps the same structure, single column, headings drop to 32px, side gutters ~24px.
- What apm borrows: neutral white/ink palette, medium-weight grotesk, 12px radius, grey-filled controls, divided group containers, one inverted dark panel per page, large air. Not borrowed: logo, name, illustrations, emoji-chip headline, copy.
